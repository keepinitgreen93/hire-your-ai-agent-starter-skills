# Request to switch on a private connection for my Product Champ account

Use this when **Settings** in your Product Champ account has no **Private Integrations** option. That option is switched on by the company that provides your account. You cannot switch it on yourself. Fill in the brackets and send it to your Product Champ support contact.

---

Hello,

I am connecting my own AI assistant to my Product Champ account so it can read my settings and help me finish setup. I do not see **Private Integrations** under **Settings** in my account.

Please do these two things for my account:

1. Switch on the Private Integrations feature for my account.
2. Allow Private Integrations for my admin user under Roles and Permissions.

My details:

- Business name: [BUSINESS NAME]
- Account login email: [YOUR LOGIN EMAIL]
- Account ID, also called Location ID, from my Business Profile page: [LOCATION ID]

What I will do once it is on:

- I will create the connection myself, inside my own account.
- It will start with view permissions only: business settings, custom values, custom fields, calendars, pipelines, forms, users, workflows, phone numbers, email templates and chat widget.
- It will not have access to contacts, conversations or payments.
- I will add an edit permission only for a change I have approved.

If you create the connection for me instead, please do not send the secret code by plain email or text. Send it with a one-time secret link that can be opened once and then expires, or tell me how to copy it myself from my account.

Thank you,
[YOUR NAME]

---

Notes for the owner:

- Keep the secret code private. Do not paste it into a chat, a screenshot or a shared document.
- You can remove this access at any time. Open **Settings, Private Integrations**, pick the connection, and delete it or rotate its code.
