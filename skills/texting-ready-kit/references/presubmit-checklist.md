# Pre-Submit Checklist (Texting Ready)

This template is a starting point, not legal advice. Have an attorney review it for your state and industry.

Phone carriers decide whether a registration is approved. Approval is never guaranteed.

Submit only once your paid plan is active. Do not start registration on a trial or before billing is on. Fees and rules change, so read the current numbers on the screen in your account before you pay.

Know who pays. If another company provides your account, ask your Product Champ support contact who is charged for registration and texting, and what happens if a charge fails. Submit only when the person paying has said yes to the cost and to the chance of rejection.

You press submit. Your AI assistant does not submit for you.

Check all ten before you press submit.

1. **Same identity everywhere.** Legal business name, DBA, address, phone and email are identical on your website, Privacy Policy, Terms and registration.
2. **Live public website.** It loads without a login and shows who you are, what you do and how to reach you. It is not "under construction."
3. **Public Privacy Policy.** One policy only, linked in the footer and on every form. No login.
4. **Non-sharing sentence.** The policy says mobile information and text consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. No wording about buying or selling lists.
5. **Public Terms.** The Terms name your business, how to STOP, how to get HELP, message frequency, "message and data rates may apply," carriers not liable, consent not a condition of purchase, and a link to your Privacy Policy.
6. **Text message checkbox done right.** Unchecked by default, optional, separate from the Terms, and it names text messages.
7. **Marketing and non-marketing split.** Two separate boxes if you send both. The box wording matches your campaign description.
8. **Message flow and samples match.** The campaign description, use case, sample messages and confirmation message tell one story, name your brand, and show STOP and HELP.
9. **Real business email.** The contact email uses your business domain, not Gmail or Yahoo. The contact person is an authorized owner or officer.
10. **Allowed content.** You do not send prohibited or high-risk content (for example third party lead generation, debt relief, gambling, or age-limited products without an age gate). Healthcare businesses have checked with counsel.

If your campaign is rejected: open the required fixes, read every one, fix the whole submission, then resubmit. Do not fix only the one line named.
